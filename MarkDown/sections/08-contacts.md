# Аннотация (англ.)

Mescheryakov A.A., Krutikov M.V., Kuprith V.U.

**The efficiency comparison of radio bearing estimation methods over rough terrain by using low directivity antennas directivity antennas**
  
The comparison results of three methods of estimating the arrival angle of HF radiation over rough terrain, which use a small-sized angle finder with wide pattern horn,log-periodic or spiral antenna having wide direction pattern, are discussed and compared.

**Keywords:** amplitude angle finder, small-sized antennas, arrival angle estimation, directional antenna pattern.
